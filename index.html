Football (Soccer)

About the Sport

Football, also known as soccer, is the world’s most popular sport played by millions across the globe. It involves two teams competing to score goals by getting the ball into the opposing team’s net. I love football because it brings people together, teaches teamwork, and requires both physical and mental skill.

Basic Rules

  • The game is played between two teams of 11 players each.
  • The match lasts for 90 minutes, divided into two halves.
  • Players cannot use their hands except for the goalkeeper.
  • A goal is scored when the ball completely crosses the goal line.
  • The team with the most goals wins the match.
